### 02. General Guidelines
1. I reserve the right to decline any commission that contains: explicit NSFW, extreme violence, discriminatory content, or anything I am uncomfortable drawing.
2. Commissions will be processed on a first come, first served basis.

---

### 03. Process & Revisions
1. Work will begin after the artist provides the initial sketch and the client confirms it.
2. Estimated turnaround time: **3–14 business days**, depending on queue and complexity.
3. Client will receive an initial sketch for approval.
4. **Unlimited revisions** are allowed during the sketching phase.
5. Once the illustration has entered the lineart phase, any revision will be subject to an **additional fee of 20% of the base price per revision**.

---

### 04. Pricing & Payment
1. Prices vary depending on the details (fullbody, bust-up, background, etc.).
2. Final pricing will be discussed and confirmed before starting.
3. Payments are made via **PayPal** or **Indonesia bank transfer / QRIS**.
4. **No refunds after the work has started.**

---

### 05. Copyright & Usage
1. I retain rights to the illustration.
2. The artwork may be used in my portfolio (unless the client specifically requests otherwise).
3. **Strictly prohibited**:

   - Using the artwork for NFTs or AI training purposes.

### 06. File Delivery
1. Final files will be delivered in **.PNG / .JPG** format (and **.CLIP** if agreed upon).
2. Files are strictly for personal use only and not for resale.

### 07. Communication & Agreement
1. All communication will be conducted via DM (Instagram/Facebook/X), Discord, or email as provided in the commission form.
